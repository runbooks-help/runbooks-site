# Third-party brand assets

## LootLocker

`lootlocker-on-dark.svg` and `lootlocker-on-light.svg` are the LootLocker
wordmark, used on the landing page's "In production at" strip. The two files are
identical except for the wordmark ink: near-white for dark backgrounds and
near-black for light ones. The parrot is full colour in both.

Used with LootLocker's permission. The LootLocker name and logo are LootLocker's
trademarks; they are not covered by this repo's FSL-1.1-MIT licence, and nothing
here grants any right to them.

Source: `player-profile-page/styles/assets/logo-darkmode.svg` (white ink) and
`logo-lightmode.svg` (dark ink) in the LootLocker repositories, copied verbatim.
