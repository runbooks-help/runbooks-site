v1.1.0 · 2026-10-08

Improvement loop

A runbook is always wrong; what matters is how fast it gets less wrong. This release builds the loop that corrects it.

  • Lookalikes. A > [!lookalike] block records what a symptom also resembles, and how to rule it out.
  • A reviewer skill. Reads a runbook and its captured notes and suggests classified changes: a step to fix, a lookalike to add, a fact to generalise, or a note that is not the runbook's job. It suggests; you edit.
  • Execution records. The captured notes and the runbook as executed, served to a remote reviewer at /api/runbooks/v1/notes.
  • Run outcomes. Completed, reopened or skipped per step across runs: described, never scored.
