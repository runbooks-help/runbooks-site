Honour mode

Supporters

The people who have bought into honour mode. The software is free and public either way: every build is open and the releases are signed. Paying is a choice, and it earns the honours, a place here and the badge for the current major.

Entries are opt-in and shown under a name of your choosing. You stay on the list once you have supported; the badge is what lapses when the next major ships.

Names appear exactly as given. We remove any that are offensive, impersonate someone, or are otherwise not a name; ask and we will remove yours too.
